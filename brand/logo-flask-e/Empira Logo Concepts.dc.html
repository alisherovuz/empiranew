1b Circuit E

It is the only concept that is both a letter and an experiment. The E reads instantly at 16px because it is three thick strokes with nothing inside them, and the amber node gives the brand a built-in "it works" moment that can animate in the product.

It maps directly to the Physics launch (batteries, bulbs, circuits) without locking Empira to one subject the way a flask points to chemistry. The spark mark is the most flexible but needs the wordmark to say "E", which weakens it as an app icon.
